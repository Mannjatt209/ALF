How Care Begins

A simple, transparent process

1. Conversation & tour. We start by listening — then you visit the home, meet caregivers, and ask anything.

2. Assessment. Washington State requires a full assessment before admission, so we understand exactly what care is needed.

3. Care plan & agreement. A negotiated care plan, disclosure of services, and a written admission agreement — so you know exactly what is provided and what it costs.

4. Welcome home. We help make move-in day calm and familiar, right down to arranging favorite keepsakes.

We accept private pay and Washington Medicaid (COPES). We're happy to walk you through the payment options for your situation.
