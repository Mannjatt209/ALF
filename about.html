About Us

A personal commitment to dignified care

LifeLine was founded on one simple belief: every adult who needs care deserves to receive it with dignity, warmth, and respect — in a real home.

Our Founder

Hello, and welcome.

My name is Manpreet Kaur, and I am the founder and licensed provider at LifeLine Adult Family Home. After years of working as a registered nurse, I opened LifeLine with one simple belief: every adult who needs care deserves to receive it with dignity, warmth, and respect — in a real home, surrounded by people who genuinely know them.

Here, caregiving isn't shift work. It's a relationship. We know every resident's story, their routines, their favorite meals, and the little things that make their day — and we treat each of them the way we would want our own parents treated.

— Manpreet Kaur, RN, Founder & Licensed Provider

Why a Small Home Matters

Five residents. One family.

Washington's Adult Family Home model exists because smaller is often better. Here's what small makes possible:

👥

More Attention

A high caregiver-to-resident ratio means no one waits long for help — and no one is just a room number.

🧑‍⚕️

Familiar Faces

Consistent caregivers who truly know each resident — not rotating shifts of strangers.

🍲

Real Home Cooking

Meals made in our own kitchen, shaped around preferences, cultures, and dietary needs.

🧘

Calm & Quiet

A low-stress residential environment that reduces anxiety and confusion — especially for those with memory changes.

❤️

Genuine Relationships

Caregivers, residents, and families become part of one another's lives. That's the point.

📍

Close to Everything

Located in Marysville, WA — near medical providers, pharmacies, and easy freeway access for visiting families.
