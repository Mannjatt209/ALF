A Different Kind of Care

Personal by design. Family at heart.

Our spacious home in Marysville is built around one idea: truly personal care. With a maximum of six residents, we give each person the time, attention, and individualized care that larger facilities simply cannot. We learn your loved one's routines, favorite meals, stories, and needs — and treat them the way we would want our own family treated.

  • High caregiver-to-resident ratio, every hour of every day
  • Consistent, familiar caregivers — not rotating shifts of strangers
  • Home-cooked meals tailored to preferences and dietary needs
  • Quiet, low-stress environment that reduces anxiety and confusion
  • True relationships between caregivers, residents, and families
